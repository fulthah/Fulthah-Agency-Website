FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with us

How long does the process take?
It typically takes us at least 3 to 11 months to help you secure a job, depending on the role, location, and your specific qualifications. We work diligently to match you with the right opportunity.
What countries do you operate in?
We operate across Europe (Germany, UK, France, Netherlands, Switzerland), the Middle East (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait), and Asia (Singapore, Japan, Malaysia, South Korea).
Do you offer visa sponsorship?
Yes, we provide comprehensive visa support for all successful candidates. Our team handles the entire visa application process to ensure a smooth transition.
What qualifications do I need?
Qualifications vary by role. We generally require relevant experience (1-6+ years depending on the position), a valid passport, and in some cases, specific certifications or language skills.
Are there any hidden fees?
No, our pricing is fully transparent. The package price covers all services from job matching to arrival support. There are no hidden fees or surprise charges.
What happens after I'm placed?
We provide 6 months of follow-up support after placement, ensuring you settle in smoothly and are happy with your new role. We're always here to help.
